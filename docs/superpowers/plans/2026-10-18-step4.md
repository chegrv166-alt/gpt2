**Goal:** Complete the playable loot/equipment/UI loop.

**Architecture:** Pure item, inventory, loot and adventure models consume existing combat events. Phaser views render drops and independent screen-space UI; GameScene coordinates input and lifecycle.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, Phaser 3.90.0, Vite, Playwright.

**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-18-step4-design.md

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies or binary/external assets; BootScene generates cached textures.
- Ten inventory slots, weapon/armor equipment, three rarities.
- Preserve existing movement, combat, forge and tests.
- Use the existing isolated checkout; no reset, commit, push or additional worktree.

## Review Focus

- Full bag must not destroy or duplicate a ground/equipped item.
- Repeated equipment swaps must not accumulate modifiers or heal.
- Pickup must respect walls, death and the paused state.
- Modal input must not reach the world, including after resize/restart.
- Boss death/death overlap and continuation must not repeatedly open a victory screen.
